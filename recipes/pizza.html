Pizza

Description

Margherita pizza is a classic Italian pizza made with a thin, crispy crust, tomato sauce, fresh mozzarella, and basil. It is simple, fresh, and flavorful, with a drizzle of olive oil to finish.

Ingredients

  • Pizza dough
  • Tomato sauce
  • Fresh mozzarella cheese
  • Fresh basil leaves
  • Olive oil
  • Salt
  • Black pepper

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 475°F (245°C).
  2. Roll out the pizza dough into a thin circle.
  3. Spread tomato sauce evenly over the dough.
  4. Add slices of fresh mozzarella cheese.
  5. Place fresh basil leaves on top.
  6. Drizzle with olive oil and season with salt and black pepper.
  7. Bake for 10–15 minutes, until the crust is golden and the cheese is melted.
  8. Let the pizza cool for a few minutes, then slice and serve.
