Lasagna

Description

Lasagna is a classic Italian dish made with layers of pasta, rich meat sauce, creamy cheese, and melted mozzarella. It is baked until the layers are hot, flavorful, and golden on top.

Ingredients

  • Lasagna noodles
  • Ground beef
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Tomato sauce
  • Tomato paste
  • Ricotta cheese
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Parmesan cheese
  • Egg
  • Olive oil
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Italian seasoning

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cook the ground beef with onion and garlic until browned.
  3. Add tomato sauce, tomato paste, salt, pepper, and Italian seasoning. Simmer for 15–20 minutes.
  4. Mix ricotta cheese with an egg and Parmesan cheese.
  5. Cook the lasagna noodles according to the package instructions.
  6. Spread a layer of meat sauce in a baking dish.
  7. Add a layer of lasagna noodles, followed by the ricotta mixture and mozzarella cheese.
  8. Repeat the layers until all the ingredients are used.
  9. Top with mozzarella and Parmesan cheese.
  10. Cover with foil and bake for 25 minutes.
  11. Remove the foil and bake for another 15–20 minutes until the cheese is golden and bubbly.
  12. Let the lasagna cool for 10–15 minutes before serving.
